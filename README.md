## About this mod

Simple Hats 9minecraft Mod adds a massive collection of cosmetic headwear to customize your player look. Every hat sits in a dedicated accessory slot so you never lose the protection of your regular helmet. You can collect hundreds of hats ranging from crowns and top hats to glowing halos and holiday gear. The mod keeps your headpieces purely visual, making it easy to show off your style in multiplayer without hurting your armor stats. Features: \t Adds hundreds of unique decorative hats to collect and wear.
